## Highlights

- **Theme picker labels are back** — on Appearance → Theme, the focused swatch's name went near-black on the dark background in beta.11/12 (the "colour picker has a black background" report). The name now brightens on focus on every theme, White included. Promised in beta.12's post — delivered.
- **Trailers on Focus keeps the title on screen** — with **Hide Titles** on, the playing tile carried no title at all. The title's logo (or its name when there's no logo art) now sits bottom-left on the tile over a soft scrim, still and while the trailer plays — the way Nuvio's own TV app does it. If your captions are visible under the cards, nothing changes (no double title).
- **Hide Discover** — Settings → Content Sources → Search Sources → **Hide Discover** hides the entire Discover section on the Search page (types, catalogs, genres and grid), leaving the search field and your recent searches. Synced per profile like the rest of your home-screen settings, and Search skips the Discover fan-out while it's hidden.
- **Card Depth "Top" you can actually see** — Edge Coverage → Top (*En haut*) drew a one-point hairline you couldn't make out from the couch, which is why "Full" looked right and "Top" looked broken. Top and Half now draw a lit-from-above rail over the top edge and corner shoulders — still no side rails, no bottom; Full is unchanged.
- **This Apple TV now shows up in your account's device list** — device registration had been rejected on every launch since the feature arrived because the app announced itself under a name the backend doesn't accept; it now registers cleanly (the platform still reads tvOS).
- Under the hood: settings toggles announce On/Off to VoiceOver; five new UI tests (theme-swatch legibility, in-tile trailer title, Hide Discover round-trip, Card Depth Top/Full, an appearance-baseline restore) plus six card-depth unit tests. Localization: French, Spanish, German, Italian and Vietnamese all carry the new strings.

### Answers to open questions
- **"Do changes on my Nuvio account propagate automatically?"** — Yes, without signing out: the Apple TV pulls everything (addons, plugins, library, watch progress, collections, home-screen catalog settings) when you pick your profile and every time the app comes back to the foreground. It does not poll while sitting open on Home — background it and re-open. Appearance preferences (theme, poster style, player settings) are per-platform by design, so phone/web changes to those don't reach the TV.
